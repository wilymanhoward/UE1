# Last Seed · Wildwood audio

The 42-second opening mix uses excerpts of the music and field recordings below. The excerpts were trimmed, faded, level-adjusted, and mixed to picture. The effects were also filtered.

## Form switch effects

Each comes from the public HQ preview, converted to mono 48 kHz PCM, with its DC offset removed, faded at both ends and peak-normalised. `Content/Python/ls_form_switch.py` makes the cuts. All three are recorded sounds, not synthesised or AI-generated audio.

- `SFX_LS_SwitchSeed.wav` layers two recordings, mixed to −8 dBFS:
  - “Rustling leaves” by Kinoton, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A rolled-up reed mat squeezed to sound like brush, recorded with a Tascam DR-60D MkII and Sennheiser ME66. The 2.05–2.75 second excerpt, faded in over 60 ms. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/Kinoton/sounds/347557/)
  - “wind chimes - single 04.wav” by Anthousai, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). One wind-chime tube struck once, recorded with a Tascam DR-40. Its first 1.2 seconds, starting 50 ms after the rustle. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/Anthousai/sounds/398496/)
- “Stone Drop on Grass 3.wav” by dichardson, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A stone dropped on grass. The whole 0.53 second recording, at −5 dBFS, is `SFX_LS_SwitchStone.wav`. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/dichardson/sounds/570976/)
- “Various short blows/puffs of air from male mouth” by rykenn, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Puffs of breath recorded for blowing dust off a camera lens. One puff, 8.33–9.05 seconds, at −8 dBFS, is `SFX_LS_SwitchDandelion.wav`. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/rykenn/sounds/432170/)

## Emberwood

- “Juggernaut” by Scott Buckley, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Human-composed dark orchestral music for strings, brass and percussion, from the composer of Wildwood’s “A Dragon’s Lullaby”. The composer's MP3 is kept as `SourceSounds/Juggernaut_ScottBuckley.mp3`. `Content/Python/ls_ember_music.py` brings it down to about −22 dBFS so it sits under the witch's voice and crossfades its last 4 seconds into its start. The result is a 218-second stereo loop that plays through all of Emberwood. [Original track](https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/juggernaut/)
- “Juggernaut” continues through the witch’s gate reaction. Its volume eases down under her dialogue and rises again as the scene ends.
- The lava ambience (`lava_amb_Cue`) is the one that comes with the Lava Environments asset pack in `Content/Lava`, used as supplied.
- The energy charge reuses the recorded storm rumble credited above. The burst cue layers “high-voltage-discharge-1.wav,” a Tesla coil recorded by follytowers, with “Huge big explosion,” an indoor firecracker recording layered by SamsterBirdies. Both are CC0. The public high-quality previews were trimmed, filtered, and mixed into `SFX_LS_EmberwoodElectricBlast.wav`. [Tesla coil](https://freesound.org/people/follytowers/sounds/415960/) [Indoor explosion](https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/591999/) [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

- The energy burst also uses the supplied `soundreality-explosion-fx-343683.mp3`. The low-frequency impact lands just after the electrical crack, and its tail recedes under the witch’s shout.
- Emberwood’s energy pickup uses a short excerpt from follytowers’ recorded Tesla coil. The spark is filtered and layered with a higher-pitched copy for the charge tail. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/follytowers/sounds/415960/) · CC0 1.0.

## Narration

- The riverbank witch dialogue uses the user-supplied ElevenLabs v4 Kristen “Cold Evil Queen Villain” take from 18 November 2026. The original MP3 is retained as `VO_LS_WitchKristen.mp3`. The recording plays at its original speed, starting 1.70 seconds into the ambush. It was filtered, level-adjusted and mixed with the river ambience and recorded scream into `SFX_LS_RiverAmbush.wav`. The scream starts at 6.95 seconds, after the dialogue. The final mix peaks at or below −3 dBFS.
- The witch's six Emberwood taunts (`VO_LS_EmberWitch_1`–`6`) use the user-supplied ElevenLabs v4 Kristen "Cold Evil Queen Villain" takes from 22 November 2026, kept as `SourceSounds/EmberWitch/VO_LS_EmberWitch_<n>_take.mp3`. The lines are in `WITCH_EMBERWOOD_LINES.md`. `Content/Python/ls_ember_witch_voice.py` puts her in a big stone hall while keeping her words clear. The voice is band-limited to 120–7000 Hz and stays in front. Two faint echoes come back at 0.42 and 0.84 seconds, and a synthetic 2.4-second hall reverb with a 45 ms pre-delay sits about 6 dB under the voice. A 3:1 compressor and a look-ahead limiter bring her speech up to about −14 dBFS, so she carries over the lava ambience. Each file is mono 48 kHz PCM, peaking at −1 dBFS, with 2.5 seconds of reverb tail.
- The Heart Tree narration uses the supplied ElevenLabs v4 take with the Adam voice. The take was split into phrases and timed to the Wildwood captions. It plays on a separate track over the recorded soundtrack.
- The Heart Tree’s final line in the Wildwood epilogue uses the supplied 23 November 2026 Adam take. A high-pass filter and peak limiter were applied before import as `VO_LS_HeartTreeEnding`.
