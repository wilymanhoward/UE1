# Last Seed · Wildwood audio

The 42-second opening mix uses excerpts of the music and field recordings below. The excerpts were trimmed, faded, level-adjusted, and mixed to picture. The effects were also filtered.

## Music

- “Heartwarming” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Original track](https://www.incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100207)
- “Dark Standoff” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Original track](https://remote.incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?Search=Search&isrc=USUAN1100268)
- “A Dragon’s Lullaby” (2023 remaster) by Scott Buckley, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Edited and level-adjusted for Wildwood’s gameplay loop and finale. [Original track](https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/a-dragons-lullaby-2023/)

## Sound effects

- “Birds forest” by Barracuda1983, released into the public domain by the recordist. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Birds_forest.ogg)
- “Thunder, Close” by Fission9, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Trimmed and filtered for the cutscene and Wildwood’s distant thunder. [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/505113/)
- “few small branches snapping, close up, high frequency,H6.wav” by Bini_trns, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/Bini_trns/sounds/354103/)
- “Witch Laugh” by mvVoiceActing, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/mvVoiceActing/sounds/223851/)
- “Tree branch_1(leaves, rustle).WAV” by o_ciz, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Trimmed and mixed for tree regrowth. [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/o_ciz/sounds/475425/)
- “Bird Whistling, Robin, Single, 13.wav” by InspectorJ (www.jshaw.co.uk), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Trimmed and mixed for tree regrowth. [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/InspectorJ/sounds/456440/)

## Narration

- The Heart Tree narration uses the supplied ElevenLabs v4 take with the Adam voice. The take was split into phrases and timed to the Wildwood captions. It plays on a separate track over the recorded soundtrack.
