# Last Seed · Wildwood audio

The 42-second opening mix uses excerpts of the music and field recordings below. The excerpts were trimmed, faded, level-adjusted, and mixed to picture. The effects were also filtered.

- “Dark Standoff” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Original track](https://remote.incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?Search=Search&isrc=USUAN1100268)

## Player movement effects

- “Wood, movement, balls, wood_balls, few, wood_marbles, small, roll, rolling, impact” by Oldome, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The recordist used a Zoom F6 and RØDE NTG5. The public HQ preview was filtered and edited into a crossfaded Seed roll loop, a short wooden takeoff tap, and a lower-pitched landing impact. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/Oldome/sounds/854006/)
- “boot scuffle small rocks 2” by oscaraudiogeek, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A boot scraping dirt and small stones, recorded with a RØDE NTG3 and Zoom H5. The public HQ preview was pitched down, filtered, and crossfaded for the Stone roll loop. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/oscaraudiogeek/sounds/328933/)
- Fission9's “Tumbling Rocks”, credited below, also supplies the Stone takeoff hit. A single impact from the public HQ preview was pitched down, filtered, and trimmed. These effects are mono 48 kHz PCM edits of recorded sounds.

## Form switch effects

Each is one excerpt of the public HQ preview, converted to mono 48 kHz PCM, with its DC offset removed, faded at both ends and peak-normalised. `Content/Python/ls_form_switch.py` makes the cuts. All three are recorded sounds, not synthesised or AI-generated audio.

- “Pop sound” by deraj, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A pop made with the lips. The 0.12–0.62 second excerpt, at −6 dBFS, is `SFX_LS_SwitchSeed.wav`. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/deraj/sounds/202230/)
- “Stone Drop on Grass 3.wav” by dichardson, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A stone dropped on grass. The whole 0.53 second recording, at −5 dBFS, is `SFX_LS_SwitchStone.wav`. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/dichardson/sounds/570976/)
- “Various short blows/puffs of air from male mouth” by rykenn, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Puffs of breath recorded for blowing dust off a camera lens. One puff, 8.33–9.05 seconds, at −8 dBFS, is `SFX_LS_SwitchDandelion.wav`. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/rykenn/sounds/432170/)

## Gorge pillar effects

- “Tumbling Rocks” by Fission9, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/488660/). The recordist describes four recordings of rocks rolling down stone stairs, captured with a Zoom H4n Pro on 12/07/2019; this is a field recording, not synthesized or AI-generated audio. The public high-quality MP3 preview was downloaded for these edits. `SFX_LS_StoneShake.wav` uses 0–0.83 seconds at 80% speed; `SFX_LS_StoneFall.wav` uses 4.12–5.40 seconds at 65% speed. Both are mono 48 kHz PCM, filtered, compressed, faded and peak-limited (−8 dBFS shaking, −5 dBFS falling). Playback adds slight pitch variation.

## Wooden bridge effects

- “Wooden creaks.wav” by HOGGanimation, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original recording](https://freesound.org/people/HOGGanimation/sounds/612694/). The recordist describes stepping on a creaky floorboard, recorded with a Zoom H5 in 2021. The public HQ preview's 0.30–1.18 second excerpt was slowed to 80%, converted to mono 48 kHz PCM, filtered, faded, level-adjusted and limited to −8 dBFS for `SFX_LS_WoodShake.wav`.
- “crate_break4” by K-Forces, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original recording](https://freesound.org/people/K-Forces/sounds/856631/). Foley recording of breaking wooden pallets in a warehouse with a Zoom H6. The public HQ preview was slowed to 80%, converted to mono 48 kHz PCM, filtered, faded, level-adjusted and limited to −5 dBFS for `SFX_LS_WoodFall.wav`. Both wood effects use recorded physical sounds, not AI synthesis.

## Riverbank ambush

- “Woman Screaming” by DanJFilms, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Human scream recorded for a short film. The public HQ preview was trimmed, filtered, level-adjusted and given a short echo for the witch's scream. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/DanJFilms/sounds/845164/)
  The repeated shockwave warning, `SFX_LS_WitchAttackScream.wav`, uses a separate 1.25-second excerpt of this recording, filtered, faded and peak-limited to −4 dBFS, in mono 48 kHz PCM.
- “flowing stream” by michelcik, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Stream recorded with a Zoom H4n in Slovakia. The public HQ preview was excerpted and faded for the riverbank ambience. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/michelcik/sounds/436512/)

## The Witch’s Exile

- The teleport cue uses the recordings already credited above for tree regrowth, witch scream, thunder and falling stone. The Emberwood bed uses “Fireplace” by myLoop under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The recordist captured an outdoor campfire in Munich using a Zoom H1essential. The public HQ preview was filtered and crossfaded for the fire loop and the end of the teleport cue. [Freesound recording](https://freesound.org/people/myLoop/sounds/852107/)
- The midair cut uses the same recorded scream, thunder, falling stone and fire loop, retimed to the witch opening the portal at 6.05 seconds.
- The planting cut combines the recorded Seed landing, tree-regrowth rustle, thunder, falling stone and Emberwood fire to follow Seed under the soil and back out into the spell.

## Narration

- The riverbank witch dialogue uses the user-supplied ElevenLabs v4 Kristen “Cold Evil Queen Villain” take from 18 November 2026. The original MP3 is retained as `VO_LS_WitchKristen.mp3`. The recording plays at its original speed, starting 1.70 seconds into the ambush. It was filtered, level-adjusted and mixed with the river ambience and recorded scream into `SFX_LS_RiverAmbush.wav`. The scream starts at 6.95 seconds, after the dialogue. The final mix peaks at or below −3 dBFS.
- The Heart Tree narration uses the supplied ElevenLabs v4 take with the Adam voice. The take was split into phrases and timed to the Wildwood captions. It plays on a separate track over the recorded soundtrack.
